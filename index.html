Bioresonance is based on modern biophysics: every cell in the body resonates at a particular frequency, and groups of cells in an organ or system form their own unique patterns.

Almost any condition will benefit from bioresonance therapy. It addresses the underlying causes of chronic and degenerative diseases. 02 · How does it work?

An example in nature: the sun

We know we "get vitamin D" from the sun. But it isn't the heat that gives us vitamin D. The sun emits a certain resonance that stimulates the body to produce vitamin D itself. When our body absorbs the rays, it isn't receiving vitamin D chemically. It's absorbing vibration from the sun.

In the same way, bioresonance sends the body specific vibrational frequencies that harmonise specific imbalanced energies.

03 · What can it do for you?

Treat the cause, not just the symptom

Instead of focusing on the symptoms of an illness, bioresonance strives to find and treat the cause of a disorder. It's designed to enhance the body's own regulation and detoxification, and to boost its natural immune system to help the body heal itself.
